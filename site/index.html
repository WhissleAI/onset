How it works

Most of the meaning arrives before you finish talking. OnSet reads it as it arrives and starts the free lookups early; the web search waits until the meaning is final.

  • Hears how you ask. Whissle Arc reads the audio itself (who is speaking, corrections, whether you're done) alongside the words.
  • Searches while you speak. The 10M-parameter Arc Tagger re-reads the partial sentence at about 0.7 ms per word, so lookups can start on word three.
  • Remembers context. A local memory graph resolves “it”, “there” and “the old one” before anything is searched.
  • Web last, and free. Cards, facts, saved answers and your recordings come first. Web search (DuckDuckGo, no key) runs only when they can't answer.
  • Answers on your Mac. A small language model writes the 1–2 sentence answer from the sources, with citations. No cloud LLM.
  • Searches your recordings. Drop in meetings, lectures or podcasts: Arc transcribes them and the Arc Tagger marks people, places and numbers, so answers can cite “Team sync · 12:40”.

Private by default

Your voice, memory, recordings and answers stay on your Mac.

Stays on your Mac
Arc (speech), the Arc Tagger, the reranker, the answer model, your indexed recordings, the memory graph, the Keystone answer store and every cache. All in ~/Library/Application Support/OnSet.
Leaves only when needed
The search query, when no local lane can answer: to DuckDuckGo, or to Tavily / Telem if you add a key. Audio goes to Deepgram only if you add a Deepgram key.
Never sent
Your memory graph, past questions, or a profile of you. Masked words never reach search, memory or logs. Keys live in the macOS Keychain.
