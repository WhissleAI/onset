How it works

Most of a question's meaning arrives before you finish asking it. OnSet starts looking as soon as it does.

  • Hears how you ask. Arc listens on your Mac and knows when you're done, so the answer is ready the moment you stop.
  • Searches while you speak. Lookups start a few words in, not after you finish.
  • Follows the conversation. Ask “how old is he?” and OnSet knows who you mean.
  • Answers on your Mac. A short, cited answer, written on your Mac. No cloud AI needed.
